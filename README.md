## 中文简介

LearnDeck 是一个面向学习内容的演示稿创作助手。它适合把课程提纲、学习笔记、文章摘要、培训资料、知识复盘或一个简单主题，整理成一套结构清楚、视觉统一、可以继续修改的演示文稿。

它关注的不是“把文件换一种格式”，而是帮助内容变得更容易讲、更容易看、更容易被学习者理解。

### 适合谁

- 老师、讲师、训练营主理人
- 知识博主、课程创作者、内容策划
- 需要做内部培训、学习汇报、知识分享的团队
- 想把零散笔记整理成正式演示材料的人

### 你会得到什么

- 一套可以直接讲解的学习演示稿
- 更清楚的章节、重点和表达节奏
- 更干净、更有质感的页面视觉
- 可以继续编辑、替换文案和复用的交付物

更多中文介绍见 [README.zh-CN.md](README.zh-CN.md)。

## English Overview

LearnDeck is a presentation companion for learning content. It helps turn course outlines, notes, article summaries, training material, knowledge reviews, or a simple topic into a clear, polished, editable presentation deck.

It is not just about producing slides. It is about shaping knowledge into a deck that is easier to teach, easier to follow, and easier to reuse.

### Who It Is For

- Teachers, trainers, mentors, and workshop hosts
- Course creators, knowledge creators, and content planners
- Teams preparing internal learning sessions or knowledge-sharing decks
- Anyone who wants to turn scattered notes into a presentation-ready deck

### What You Get

- A presentation deck ready for teaching or sharing
- A clearer learning flow with sections, highlights, and takeaways
- A polished visual style that makes ideas easier to scan
- An editable deliverable you can refine and reuse
